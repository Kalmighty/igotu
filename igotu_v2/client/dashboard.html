How can we help?

Practical answers for your Client workspace.

How do I create a service request?

Choose Create a Service Request, describe the help you need and enter your preferred date, time and other requested details.

How does IGotU recommend suitable Providers?

IGotU uses your request to recommend eligible Providers. Open a request from My Requests to review the recommendations and compare the information shown. A recommendation does not guarantee Provider availability.

What happens after I select a Provider?

The selected Provider can confirm or decline the assignment. Selection is not confirmation. Check your request’s current status before making service arrangements.

Where can I track my service request?

Open My Requests and choose the request to see its details, selected Provider and current service status. The dashboard shows only your most recent requests.

How do I contact my selected Provider?

Use Messages to open the conversation for your selected Provider when it is available through the existing service workflow.

How are offline payments recorded?

Pay the Provider offline. Open the request’s details from My Requests to record payment information when the payment form is available, including any proof the form requires. IGotU records payment information; it does not collect or process payments here. Reviews remain available according to the existing completed-service workflow.

How can I view my identity-verification status?

Choose Identity Verification in your account dropdown or open your verification page to view your current status. Mandatory identity approval and profile-completion requirements remain unchanged.
