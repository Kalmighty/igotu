How It Works

  1. Describe your request

    Provide service details, your preferred date and time, and the service location.

  2. Review suitable Providers

    IGotU recommends eligible Providers based on the matching criteria.

  3. Select and wait for confirmation

    Choose a Provider. The Provider can then confirm or decline the assignment.

Tips for a Better Match

  • Use a clear service title.
  • Choose the correct category and specialisation.
  • Describe the required work accurately.
  • Select the correct location.
  • Provide a suitable preferred date and time.
  • Enter a realistic budget when appropriate.
